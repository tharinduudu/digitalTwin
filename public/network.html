What does recent mean?

Station records and locations

The Network summary contains separate detector IDs, including similarly named and possibly duplicate records. None have been merged. USA and United States count as one country here. Nearby records share a map pin; selecting it lists them individually. Locations come from the portal map and are not surveyed coordinates.

What is not measured here

This snapshot contains no individual particle times or current count rates. There are no invented station pulses, intercontinental particle tracks or simulated replacements for missing data. Use the portal for the latest measurements. Data status and stability are the portal's reported assessments at capture time, not new diagnoses.
